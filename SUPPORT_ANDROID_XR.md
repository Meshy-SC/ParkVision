# ParkVision Support (Android XR)

**Welcome to ParkVision Support.** We are here to help you get the most out of your puzzle experience on Android XR.

## Supported Devices

Android XR headsets, such as Samsung Galaxy XR, with the app installed from Google Play.

ParkVision is a mixed-reality game: the parking lot appears on the floor of your own room, over the headset's view of the room. Play standing or seated, with your hands.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I play?**
Your car is the one on the glowing amber pad, and it has to reach the exit. Every other vehicle can only roll forward or back in its own lane. Slide them out of the way, one move at a time, until your car can drive out.

**How do I move a vehicle?**
Look or point at it, pinch, and drag it along its lane. Let go to drop it: it snaps to the nearest space, or stops when something blocks it.

**How do I choose a level?**
ParkVision opens on the level browser, a window in your space. Pick a world, then a level, and the lot appears on the floor in front of you. The level-selection button on the toolbar, or the Back gesture, brings you back to the browser.

**What do the toolbar buttons do?**
From left to right: level selection, restart the level, solution (plays the solution step by step), undo the last move, previous level and next level. The move counter shows your moves against par. When the next level is locked, its button is dimmed: select it to see why.

**The lot is in the wrong place.**
Pinch the white bar on the floor at the lot's near edge and drag it: the lot follows your hand across the floor and turns to face you.

**Why does ParkVision ask to scan my room and track my head?**
Both are optional. With the room scan, the game uses the height of your floor to stand the lot on it. With the head position, it puts the lot in front of you and turns the level-complete card to face you. Nothing leaves your device. If you refuse, the lot opens in front of where you stood when the app opened, on the game's estimate of the floor, and the bar moves it. You can change your answer in the headset's settings.

**Can I use a keyboard?**
With a keyboard connected: Z undoes the last move, R restarts the level, Escape returns to the level browser. Vehicles are moved by pinching and dragging.

### Worlds, Stars & Scoring

**How many levels are there?**
1,000 puzzles in ten worlds: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend, on 6×6, 7×7 and 8×8 lots.

**How do I unlock levels?**
Inside a world, levels come in groups of ten: complete six levels of a group to unlock the next one. Worlds are never locked by progress: once you own a world, you can start it right away.

**How do I earn 3 stars?**
Finish a level in par, the fewest moves possible. Within 20% above par earns two stars, and any other finish one star. Stars are worth 10, 7 and 5 points toward your score.

### Free Levels and Purchases

**What is free?**
ParkVision is free to download. The first ten levels of the Iron world, and the first level of each of the other nine worlds, are free to play.

**How do I unlock a whole world?**
Each world is a one-time purchase that unlocks all 100 of its levels, and one purchase unlocks all ten worlds at once. Buy them from the banner above a world's levels, when the game offers the next world after a free level, or in **Settings → Purchases**. Purchases go through Google Play.

**I bought single worlds and then All Worlds.**
The All Worlds purchase opens every world, including the ones you already own; earlier purchases of single worlds are not refunded automatically. If you bought it by mistake, ask Google Play for a refund or contact us.

**What are solution credits?**
The solution button plays a level's solution. One credit unlocks a level's solution for good: replay it as often as you like. Completing a level does not unlock its solution; that takes a credit too. You get one credit free; packs of 1, 5, 10 and 25 credits are in **Settings → Purchases**, or offered when you press the solution button with no credit left.

**Do my purchases survive a reinstall?**
Worlds do: they belong to your Google account, and the game finds them again when it opens (or with **Settings → Restore Purchases**). Solution credits you have not used, and the solutions you unlocked, are stored on the device only: uninstalling the app or clearing its storage loses them, unless Android's backup restores the app's data.

**My purchase is missing.**
Make sure the headset is online and signed in to the Google account that made the purchase, then open **Settings → Restore Purchases**. If it is still missing, contact us (see below).

### Settings

**Where are the settings?**
In the level browser, open **Settings**. You can turn the background music and each sound effect on or off, buy solution credits or all ten worlds, restore purchases, open this page and the privacy policy, reset your progress, and see the app's version.

### Data and Privacy

**What data does ParkVision collect?**
None. Your progress, settings and solution credits are stored on the device only. Purchases go through Google Play, which tells the app, on your device, which add-ons your Google account owns. ParkVision has no servers, ads or analytics. For full details, including how to delete your data, see our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_ANDROID_XR.md).

**How do I start over?**
Open **Settings → Reset All Progress**. This permanently deletes all level completions, star ratings and scores. Your purchases and solution credits are kept.

### Troubleshooting

**The lot floats above the floor or sinks into it.**
Without the room scan the game estimates where your floor is. Allow the room scan in the headset's settings (Apps → ParkVision → Permissions) and open the level again.

**There is no sound.**
Check the switches in **Settings** and the headset's volume.

**The app is crashing or not responding.**
1. Close the app and reopen it.
2. Make sure your headset's system software is up to date.
3. Update ParkVision to the latest version from Google Play.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below).
