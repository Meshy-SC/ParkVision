# ParkVision Support (Meta Quest)

**Welcome to ParkVision Support.** We are here to help you get the most out of your puzzle experience on Meta Quest.

## Supported Devices

- **Meta Quest 2**
- **Meta Quest Pro**
- **Meta Quest 3**
- **Meta Quest 3S**

ParkVision is a mixed-reality game: the parking lot appears on the floor of your own room, through the headset's passthrough. Play standing or seated, with Touch controllers or with your hands.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I play?**
Your car is the one on the glowing amber pad, and it has to reach the exit. Every other vehicle can only roll forward or back in its own lane. Slide them out of the way, one move at a time, until your car can drive out.

**How do I move a vehicle?**
Point at it, then hold the trigger (controllers) or pinch (hands) and drag it along its lane. Let go to drop it: it snaps to the nearest space, or stops when something blocks it.

**How do I choose a level?**
ParkVision opens on the level browser. Pick a world, then a level, and the lot appears in front of you. The level-selection button on the toolbar brings you back to the browser.

**What do the toolbar buttons do?**
From left to right: level selection, restart the level, solve (plays the solution step by step), undo the last move, previous level and next level. The move counter shows your moves against par.

### Worlds, Stars & Scoring

**How many levels are there?**
1,000 puzzles in ten worlds: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend, on 6×6, 7×7 and 8×8 lots.

**How do I unlock levels?**
Every world is open from the start. Inside a world, levels come in groups of ten: complete six levels of a group to unlock the next one.

**How do I earn 3 stars?**
Finish a level in par, the fewest moves possible. Within 20% above par earns two stars, and any other finish one star. Stars are worth 10, 7 and 5 points toward your score.

### Settings

**Where are the settings?**
In the level browser, open **Settings**. You can turn the background music and each sound effect on or off, open this page and the privacy policy, reset your progress, and see the App's version.

### Data and Privacy

**What data does ParkVision collect?**
None. Your progress and settings are stored on the headset only. For full details, see our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_MH.md).

**How do I start over?**
Open **Settings → Reset All Progress**. This permanently deletes all level completions, star ratings and scores.

### Troubleshooting

**The App closes as soon as a level opens, or the lot does not appear.**
The headset needs to track its position in the room. Make sure the room is well lit and the headset's cameras are not covered, then open the level again.

**My hands do not move the vehicles.**
Make sure hand tracking is turned on in the headset's settings, and keep your hand in view of the headset while you pinch.

**There is no sound.**
Check the switches in **Settings** and the headset's volume.

**The App is crashing or not responding.**
1. Close the App and reopen it.
2. Make sure your headset is running the latest Horizon OS version.
3. Update ParkVision to the latest version from the Meta Horizon Store.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below).
