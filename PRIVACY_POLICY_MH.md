# Privacy Policy for ParkVision on Meta Quest

**Last Updated:** November 5, 2026

## Overview

ParkVision ("the App") is a parking-lot sliding puzzle game for Meta Horizon OS, available on Meta Quest 2, Meta Quest Pro, Meta Quest 3 and Meta Quest 3S. It is developed and published by Meshy ("we", "our", "us"). This policy covers the Meta Quest version of the App.

## Data Collection

ParkVision does **not** collect, store or transmit any personal data to us. We operate no servers, no databases and no back end of any kind, and we have no access to your information.

### We Do NOT Collect
- Personal identification information
- Location data
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Advertising identifiers
- Crash reports
- Camera images, room data or recordings
- Any data whatsoever

## Data Stored on Your Headset

To keep your game going, the App saves the following **on your headset only**, in its private app storage:

- Level completions, best move counts, star ratings and scores
- Your music and sound-effect settings

None of this is sent to us. You can erase your progress at any time from **Settings → Reset All Progress**, and uninstalling the App removes all of it from the headset.

**Meta cloud backup.** If cloud backup is turned on in your headset's settings, Horizon OS may include this saved progress in the backup Meta keeps for your account, so that it can be restored on another headset. That backup is run by Meta, not by us, and Meta's privacy policy applies to it: [meta.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/)

## Mixed Reality, Controllers and Hands

- **Passthrough.** The App places the puzzle in your room using the headset's passthrough view. Horizon OS draws the passthrough image itself: the App never receives, stores or transmits camera images or information about your room.
- **Controllers and hand tracking.** While you play, the App reads the position of your controllers or hands from Horizon OS so that you can point at and drag the vehicles. This is used on the spot and is never stored or transmitted.

## Third-Party Services

### Meta Horizon Platform
The App uses the Meta Horizon Platform SDK only to check that your copy of the App was obtained through the Meta Horizon Store (an entitlement check the store requires). The check is handled by Meta's software on your headset, and Meta's privacy policy applies to it: [meta.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/)

The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no in-app purchases. It needs no internet connection to play.

## Data Sharing

We do **not**:
- Sell your data.
- Share your data with third parties.
- Use your data for advertising.
- Track your behaviour across apps or sessions.

## Permissions Requested

- **Hand tracking** — lets you play with your hands instead of controllers.

ParkVision does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, files outside its own storage, or any advertising identifier.

## Children's Privacy

ParkVision does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes are published constitutes acceptance.
