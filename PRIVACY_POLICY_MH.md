# ParkVision Privacy Policy — Meta Quest

- **App:** ParkVision (on the Meta Horizon Store)
- **Publisher:** Meshy (Ahmed Dalhi)
- **Last updated:** November 10, 2026

---

## Summary

ParkVision has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. Your progress and settings stay on your headset. We cannot see them.

ParkVision is free to download and sells add-ons through the Meta Horizon Store. For this, it uses two Meta Horizon platform features, which involve data from your Meta account:

- your **Meta user ID** for this app (Meta's "User ID" feature)
- your **ParkVision purchases** (Meta's "In-app purchases and/or downloadable content" feature)

The app uses this data only to unlock what you bought and to restore it. It also checks with Meta that your copy of the app came from the Meta Horizon Store. Any user can ask us to delete their data at any time. Section 7 explains how.

## 2. Data We Collect and How We Use It

### 2.1 Your Meta user ID (Meta "User ID" feature)

- **What it is:** the number Meta uses to identify your Meta account inside ParkVision. It is not your username. Meta gives each app a different number for the same person, so this one cannot identify you in other apps.
- **How it is used:** when ParkVision asks Meta which add-ons you own, opens Meta's checkout, or marks a solution-credit pack as delivered, the Meta Horizon Platform SDK makes the request for your signed-in Meta account. Meta uses your user ID to find your purchases and to record new ones.
- The app itself does not read, display, save or send this ID. It is used only between Meta Horizon OS and Meta's servers.

### 2.2 Your ParkVision purchases (Meta "In-app purchases and/or downloadable content" feature)

The first ten levels of the Iron world and the first level of every other world are free. In the app you can buy:

- each of the ten worlds (Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend), which unlocks all of its levels for good
- all ten worlds at once, in one purchase
- packs of solution credits; one credit unlocks one level's solution for good

What happens:

- **What the app receives from Meta:** the prices of these add-ons in your currency, the list of add-ons your Meta account owns, and the identifier of each solution-credit pack you buy.
- **How it is used:** to open the worlds you own, to add the credits of each pack exactly once, and to restore your purchases after a reinstall or on another headset (**Settings → Restore Purchases**).
- **Payment** happens entirely in Meta's own checkout. We never see or receive your payment details, your name, your email address or your billing address.

### 2.3 Ownership check (entitlement)

When the app starts, it asks Meta Horizon OS whether your Meta account is entitled to ParkVision, as the Meta Horizon Store requires. It gets back a yes or no answer, which is used only for this check and is not saved or sent anywhere.

### 2.4 Mixed reality, controllers and hands

- **Passthrough.** The app places the parking lot on the floor of your room using the headset's passthrough view. Meta Horizon OS draws the passthrough image itself: the app never receives, saves or sends camera images or information about your room.
- **Head position.** To draw the pointer from your controller or hand, and to open the level-complete and purchase cards where you are looking, the app reads the headset's position from Meta Horizon OS while you play. It is used on the spot and is never saved or sent.
- **Controllers and hands.** The app reads your Touch controllers' buttons and pointing direction, or, if you play with your hands, the position of your hands and your pinch, so that you can point at the vehicles, drag them and press buttons. This is used on the spot and is never saved or sent.

### 2.5 Emails you send us

If you email us for support or to request deletion, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you include. We use it only to answer you or to handle your request. Your email passes through our email provider. We keep it only while we handle your request. For deletion requests, we delete your email once we have confirmed the deletion to you.

## 3. Data That Stays on Your Headset

To keep your game going, the app saves the following in its private storage on your headset. **It never leaves your headset (except in Meta's cloud backup, below), and we cannot see it.**

- Level completions, best move counts, star ratings and scores
- Your music and sound-effect settings
- Your solution credits, the levels whose solution you unlocked, and the identifiers of the credit packs already added, so that a pack is never added twice
- The add-ons your Meta account owns: a copy of Meta's answer, so the game also works offline

**Meta cloud backup.** If cloud backup is turned on in your headset's settings, Meta Horizon OS may include this data in the backup Meta keeps for your Meta account, so that it can be restored on another headset. Meta runs that backup under [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

## 4. Data We Do Not Collect

We collect nothing beyond what sections 2 and 3 describe. In particular, ParkVision does **not** collect:

- Your name, email address or phone number (unless you email us, as in section 2.5)
- Your payment details
- Hardware serial numbers or advertising identifiers
- Location, whether precise or approximate
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Camera images, room data, recordings or voice

The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting code. If the app crashes, Meta Horizon OS may report it to Meta, and Meta's developer dashboard then shows us the technical details: the headset model, the Meta Horizon OS and app versions, and where in the code the crash happened.

## 7. Requesting Data Deletion

**All users, in every country, can ask us to delete their data at any time. It is free, and you do not have to give a reason.**

### Data on your headset

You can delete it yourself, immediately:

- In ParkVision, **Settings → Reset All Progress** deletes your level completions, best move counts, star ratings and scores. Your purchases and solution credits are kept.
- **Uninstalling ParkVision** permanently deletes all of the app's data from your headset. World purchases stay on your Meta account: if you install the app again, **Settings → Restore Purchases** brings them back.

### Data held by Meta

Your purchases are part of your Meta account, and Meta keeps them so that you can restore them. You can manage or delete the data Meta holds about your Meta account with Meta's own tools, described in [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/). We hold no copy of this data.

### Ask us

Email **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** with the subject **"Data deletion request"**. Within **30 days**, we delete any emails you have sent us, help you remove the data described above, and confirm the deletion to you by email.

We will not refuse a deletion request. No law requires us to keep any of this data.

## 10. System Permissions

- **Internet (`INTERNET`):** used only by the Meta Horizon Platform SDK, to reach Meta for purchases and the ownership check.
- **Hand tracking (`com.oculus.permission.HAND_TRACKING`, `horizonos.permission.HAND_TRACKING`):** lets you play with your hands instead of controllers (section 2.4).

ParkVision does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, files outside its own storage, or any advertising identifier.
